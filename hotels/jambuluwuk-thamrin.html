Jambuluwuk Thamrin Hotel is a four-star hotel located along Jalan Riau in the Gondangdia area of Menteng, Central Jakarta, Indonesia. The hotel is located in a busy city area, surrounded by roads, offices, houses, and other buildings. Open spaces within the property are limited and mainly include the hotel entrance, vehicle access area, terraces, and the area around the swimming pool.

The hotel has a vertical building arrangement, with guest rooms located on the upper floors and public facilities placed on different levels. The lobby, restaurants, meeting rooms, guest service areas, and recreational facilities are located within the main hotel building.

The hotel provides guest rooms, restaurants, meeting and ballroom facilities, wellness facilities, and other guest services. The information on this page is based on what was observed during the stay, together with publicly available information. No hotel staff were interviewed, and no technical documents were reviewed.

2. Location
2.1. Address

Jambuluwuk Thamrin Hotel is located at Jl. Riau No.5, RT.9/RW.7, Gondangdia, Kec. Menteng, Kota Jakarta Pusat, Daerah Khusus Ibukota Jakarta 10350, Indonesia

2.2. Frontage Road Condition

Jalan Riau is a two-way asphalt road with an estimated total width of around 4 metres. The road has one lane in each direction and no centre median. There is no designated pedestrian path, so people mainly walk along the road edge or over the covered drainage beside the properties.

2.3. Access to the Hotel

Jambuluwuk Thamrin Hotel is located on Jalan Riau, between Jalan Lombok and Jalan Irian . Jalan Lombok is a one-way street. Jalan Riau and Jalan Irian also operate as one-way streets on weekdays between 6:00 am and 6:00 pm. This traffic rule does not apply on public holidays.

Jambuluwuk Thamrin Hotel is located approximately 1 kilometre from Bundaran HI or around 10–15 minutes on foot.

How to reach the Hotel
  • From Soekarno Hatta International Airport :Visitors arriving by plane may follow Jalan C1, Jalan C2, and Jalan Raya Bandara Soekarno–Hatta before entering the Airport Toll Road. The route continues through the Prof. Dr. Ir. Sedyatmo Toll Road and the Inner-City Toll Road, before exiting toward Bendungan Hilir, Senayan, or Kebayoran. From there, continue along Jalan Gatot Subroto and Jalan Jenderal Sudirman toward Bundaran HI.
    From Bundaran HI, the route continues toward Jalan H. Agus Salim, then turns into Jalan Lombok and continues to Jalan Riau, where Jambuluwuk Thamrin Hotel is located.
    Travel time by car is usually around 45–75 minutes, depending on traffic conditions.
    Other routes option in Google Maps .

  • From Halim Perdanakusuma International Airport: Visitors arriving by plane may follow Jalan Raya Halim Perdanakusuma and Jalan Letjen M.T. Haryono before entering the Cawang–Grogol Inner-City Toll Road.
    Take the exit toward Mampang, Kuningan, or Menteng, then continue through Jalan Gatot Subroto, Jalan H. R. Rasuna Said, and Jalan HOS Cokroaminoto. From there, turn into Jalan Prof. Moh. Yamin, continue to Jalan H. Agus Salim, then enter Jalan Lombok and Jalan Riau, where Jambuluwuk Thamrin Hotel is located.

  • From Gambir Station: Visitors arriving by train may follow Jalan Medan Merdeka Timur, Jalan Medan Merdeka Utara, and Jalan Medan Merdeka Barat. The route then continues through Jalan Sunda before turning into Jalan Timor and Jalan Riau, where Jambuluwuk Thamrin Hotel is located.

  • By MRT: Visitors using the Jakarta MRT may get off at Bundaran HI MRT Station. From the station, continue toward Jalan H. Agus Salim, then enter Jalan Lombok and Jalan Riau, where Jambuluwuk Thamrin Hotel is located. The walk takes around 10–15 minutes.

3. Surrounding Facilities

Surrounding facilities are grouped by practical guest access from the property: walkable facilities within approximately 10 minutes on foot, and short-drive facilities within approximately 5 minutes by vehicle.

Note on Walking Routes: Some facilities across Jalan M.H. Thamrin, including Plaza Indonesia, Grand Hyatt, and Four Points, are closer than the walking route shown by Google Maps. Visitors can use the Bundaran HI MRT underground pedestrian tunnel or the Sarinah pedestrian bridge to cross the main road. The actual walking time is about 5 to 10 minutes, although Google Maps may show a route of around 17 to 22 minutes.
