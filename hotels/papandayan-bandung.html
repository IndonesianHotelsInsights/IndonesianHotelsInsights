1. Property Description

The Papandayan is a five-star hotel located along Jl. Gatot Subroto, commonly known as Gatsu Bandung, in Bandung, West Java, Indonesia. The property combines hotel buildings with landscaped open spaces within the site. Several outdoor and semi-open areas can be observed around the property, including garden areas, terraces, open walkways connecting different sections of the hotel, and large recreational areas surrounding the swimming pool.

The hotel is characterised by a low- to mid-rise building arrangement, with guest rooms, restaurants, meeting facilities, and recreational areas distributed across multiple connected sections. Several public facilities and guest areas are separated across different parts of the property rather than concentrated within a single building structure.

The hotel provides guest accommodation, food and beverage outlets, meeting and ballroom facilities, wellness facilities, and supporting guest services. Observations and descriptions are based on publicly available information and limited physical inspection during the stay period, without verification through stakeholder interviews or technical documentation.

2. Location
2.1. Address

Papandayan Hotel is located at Jalan Gatot Subroto No. 83, Kelurahan Malabar, Kecamatan Lengkong, Bandung, West Java 40262.

2.2. Frontage Road Condition

The frontage road is a two-way urban street with an approximate width of 6 metres for both directions combined. The road utilises asphalt pavement construction without a central median, with one traffic lane provided for each direction. Pedestrian pathways and closed drainage systems were also observed along portions of the road frontage.

2.3. Access to the Hotel

The Papandayan Hotel is located on Jalan Gatot Subroto, between Gedung Pensil / Simpang Lima area and the intersection of Jalan Gatot Subroto and Jalan Malabar. The property is positioned approximately 2.0 kilometres from Alun-Alun Bandung / Bandung City Square, depending on the route used.

When travelling eastward from Gedung Pensil, the hotel is located on the left side of the road; when approaching from the Jalan Gatot Subroto–Jalan Malabar intersection, the hotel is located on the right side.

How to reach the Hotel
  • From Husein Sastranegara Airport: Visitors arriving by plane may use this fastest route options pass through Jalan Pajajaran, Jalan Veteran, Jalan A. Yani before continuing toward Jalan Gatot Subroto where the hotel is located. Other routes option in Google Maps

  • From Bandung Railway Station : The common route is Jl. Stasiun Timur, Jl. Perintis Kemerdekaan, Jl. Lembong, Jl. Veteran, Jl. A. Yani, and continue toward Jl Gatot Subroto.
    Visitors using the Whoosh high-speed rail from Jakarta usually arrive at Padalarang Station, then continue by feeder train to Bandung Railway Station before travelling by road to the hotel. Other routes option in Google Maps
    Ticketing and schedule information may be checked through the official Whoosh / KCIC ticketing website .

  • From Leuwipanjang Bus Terminal : Visitors arriving by intercity bus may use this route options pass through Jalan Leuwi Panjang, Jl. BKR, Jl. Moch. Ramdan turn right to Jl. Emong, Jl. Burangrang and entered to Jl. Gatot Subroto. Other routes option in Google Maps

Nearby Experiences

The nearby experiences shown here were documented during the stay at The Papandayan. They are included as part of the surrounding visitor experience, based on places that were personally visited, photographed, or recorded. Salomon Concept Store

Salomon Concept Store is located along Jalan Gatot Subroto and was documented as one of the nearby experiences during the stay at The Papandayan. The store adds a modern retail element to the surrounding street environment, particularly for visitors interested in outdoor, running, or lifestyle products. The observation was limited to the visible shop setting and the experience of visiting the store from the hotel area.

Specialized

Specialized is located next to the Salomon Concept Store along Jalan Gatot Subroto. The documentation focuses on the visible store frontage, retail setting, and its relationship to the surrounding Jalan Gatot Subroto environment.
